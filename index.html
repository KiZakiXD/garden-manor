The Manor House · Jakarta — 2027

Dearest family and friends,

Some letters carry news too joyful to stay sealed for long — and this is one of them. After seasons of laughter, patience, and quietly answered prayers, we are overjoyed to write to you with the happiest of announcements: we are getting married.

What began as an ordinary introduction has grown, page by page, into a story we never wish to put down — two families becoming one, and two hearts choosing, day after day, to walk in the same direction.

With the Blessing of God Almighty

Our Story

Written Along the Way

01

The First Page

Every great letter begins with a single, unremarkable line — ours was a simple introduction on an ordinary afternoon. No grand gestures, no written plans; only two strangers who kept finding reasons to continue the conversation long after it should have ended.

02

The Long Correspondence

Season followed season, and the pages kept filling: laughter shared over small things, burdens carried two-by-two, and quiet prayers whispered for one another in the stillness of the night. Somewhere between the lines, friendship learned to spell itself differently.

03

The Next Chapter

Now, with steady hearts and a clear intention, we turn the page together. This wedding is not the end of our story but its most beautiful beginning — a home built on faith, tenderness, and mercy, for all the chapters still to come. Aamiin.
